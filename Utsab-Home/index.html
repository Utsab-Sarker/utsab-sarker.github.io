About me

I am Utsab Sarker, currently living in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My father is Uttam Kumar Sarkar, and my mother is Shima Rani Pramanik. Although I now live in Dhaka, a significant part of my educational journey was spent in Rajshahi, where I completed my studies up to Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC). After that, I moved to Dhaka to pursue higher education, gain experience, and develop my professional skills.
